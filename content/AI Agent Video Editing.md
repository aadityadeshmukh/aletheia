# The Code-Based Frame: AI Agent Video Editing

The traditional video editing workflow—a tedious choreography of dragging clips across a timeline—is being superseded by a code-based generative model. Frameworks like **[[Remotion]]** allow for the programmatic creation of video, which, when coupled with AI agents, enables a "zero-asset" production environment. In this paradigm, video is not edited; it is describe into existence, ensuring perfect alignment with a brand's technical and aesthetic DNA.

### Analysis
- **Code vs. Timeline:** By defining video through a React-based codebase rather than a visual timeline, the creator achieves surgical precision and programmatic scalability that traditional tools like Premiere cannot match.
- **Brand System Integration:** AI agents can audit an existing web application or design system prior to production, ensuring that the generated video natively adopts the fonts, icons, and color tokens of the host brand.
- **The Vision Over Technicality:** The shift removes the technical barrier to entry; what matters most is the clarity of the vision and the ability to express it simply, as the "hard part" of screen-rendering is outsourced to the agent.

### Synthesis
The gap between the idea and the export is disappearing. **In the age of AI, the prompt is the new production studio.**
